# Diagnosis verdict

After you look, do not stop on the investigation.

1. Say whether it is a bug. Answer yes or no before you stop. "Everything looks normal" is a valid no. Do not end on a maybe.
2. If it is a bug, name the remedy in the same reply. State what to change. Do not wait to be asked what to fix.
